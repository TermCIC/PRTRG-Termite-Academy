NPMA — practical control training

Budgeted 20,000

Northern Pest Management Association (NPMA). Request letter sent 15 November 2026; reply requested by 15 December 2026. Ask about partnership before asking for a price — the day puts their trainers in front of 40 regional professionals. Day 3 is a whole day of training, and they arrange the teaching. We ask for four things: the equipment explained, a short course on methods and precautions, outdoor injection, and the underground reticulation pipes uncovered so the pipe run can be seen.
