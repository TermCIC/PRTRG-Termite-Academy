01 — The ask

Five decisions are requested tonight

Lecturers, programme and field plan are far enough advanced to proceed. What blocks the announcement is money and two partnership terms. The field day is at the Mushroom Research Center in Pa Pae, and the President has required a complete collecting kit for every participant. The core cost is THB 186,973. D1 asks the board to confirm USD 200, which covers it with real headroom. D3 asks the board to name the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology, Chiang Mai University as co-organiser. D4 asks for a written Mushroom Research Center site and publication agreement before fieldwork. Registration and payment are being handled outside the board, by a dedicated webpage — see §04.

D1

Confirm the fee at USD 200

What the fee has to cover
A core cost of THB 186,973, built from published or quoted rates wherever possible: transport, food, the collecting kit for every participant (which includes printing, certificates and lab materials), the training day, both dinners, safety cover, helper wages, and the Mushroom Research Center.
Recommendation
USD 200 from everyone, students and lecturers alike, excluding accommodation. At a full house this leaves THB 17,027, and stays positive down to 12 of the 15 non-student places — three can go unsold.
What a student pays
THB 6,800 on registration, less the THB 2,720 grant, plus THB 755 for three nights in a shared dormitory — about THB 4,835, roughly USD 142 all in — and they keep a collecting kit worth THB 650.
If the board wants more headroom
USD 220 leaves THB 44,227 and needs only 7 non-student places; USD 230 leaves THB 57,827 and needs 5.
Two things the board should know
Each invited lecturer pays the full fee while teaching without honorarium and paying their own airfare and accommodation; the invitations must say so plainly and early. And the practical control training line of THB 20,000 assumes a company partners in exchange for being named — at full commercial rates that component is THB 33,500 to 68,500.
D1b

Approve the student grant

Recommendation
USD 80 per student, 25 students, USD 2,000 in total — about THB 68,000. Paid to each student at the close of the Academy, as a participation grant.
Where the money comes from
Registration income covers it. At USD 200 a full house takes THB 272,000 against a core cost of THB 186,973, which leaves THB 85,027 — enough for the grants with THB 17,027 to spare. The society is not being asked for new money, but it is being asked to accept that the surplus is spoken for.
Decide what happens if the Academy does not fill
This is the real question in D1b. At USD 200, four unsold non-student places put the Academy about THB 6,700 short; at 31 — the students and the lecturers alone — it is THB 25,650 short. The board should say now whether the shortfall falls on PRTRG, on sponsorship, or on the grant itself — deciding it in April, after students have been recruited on the promise, would be much worse.
Mechanism, and why the timing matters
Everyone is invoiced USD 200 and pays in full at registration. A PRTRG representative hands each of the 25 students USD 80 at the close of the Academy. Nothing is netted off up front, which keeps the accounting simple and the society’s support visible. It also means PRTRG holds the whole THB 272,000 through the event and pays out only at the end — the money is in hand before it is spent — and a student who registers but does not attend never collects the grant.
D2

Registration timetable and refund policy

Decision needed
When registration opens and closes, and what happens if a participant withdraws or the Academy is cancelled.
Recommendation
Registration opens October 2026 and closes 28 February 2027. Full refund if the Academy is cancelled by the organisers. For participant withdrawal: full refund before 31 March 2027, no refund after, because catering, transport, and accommodation are committed by then.
What the board should know
The February deadline is set by visa processing, not by convenience. International participants need roughly six to eight weeks to obtain a Thai visa with an invitation letter, and the letters cannot be issued before payment is confirmed.
D3

Name the CMU department as co-organiser

Decision needed
Whether the Academy should publicly credit the CMU academic partner as a joint organiser.
Recommendation
Approve the wording: “Jointly organised by the Pacific Rim Termite Research Group and the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology, Chiang Mai University.”
Where it appears
The announcement, registration page, participant handbook, certificates, backdrop or banners, reports, and other official Academy materials.
Department head
Assoc. Prof. Dr. Ratchadawan Cheewangkoon, Head of the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology, has agreed to give the Day 1 welcome — a working signal of the department’s support, though not yet the written confirmation below.
Before publication
Obtain written confirmation of the wording from Dr. Cheewangkoon as the department’s authorised representative, and of the lecture-room, laboratory, audiovisual and local academic support included in the arrangement.
D4

Set the Mushroom Research Center research-output terms

Decision needed
Whether the organisers may conclude a written agreement for use of this private field venue before any sampling begins.
The relationship is now more than a venue booking
Dr. Ishika Bera of the Center is a confirmed Day 1 lecturer, speaking on termite-mushroom diversity in Thailand — see §03. That gives the Center a direct stake in the Academy beyond hosting Day 2, which should make the terms below easier to agree.
Recommendation
Kevin D. Hyde should be invited into the research design, interpretation and manuscript review for papers using Mushroom Research Center data or specimens. Where his documented contribution meets the target journal’s authorship criteria, he is included as a co-author; otherwise Kevin D. Hyde and the Mushroom Research Center are acknowledged.
Put in writing
Site access, sampling permission, specimen and data custody, permitted research uses, publication review, expected contributions, and the process for deciding author order.
Integrity safeguard
Access to a private venue is not, by itself, automatic authorship. Final authorship must reflect actual scholarly contribution and the policy of the journal receiving the paper.

04 — Reporting item

Programme and registration model

Three-day programme, hour by hour Drafted CMU Faculty of Agriculture hosts Day 1 and the Day 3 closing; transport otherwise runs direct, with no stop there. Day 1 is seven 45-minute lecture sessions — shortened and multiplied at the President’s request, all seven now named — closing with a 15-minute Day 2 briefing and the welcome dinner at Galae Restaurant, after which minibuses take everyone to Mae Hia for the first time. Day 2 is the Pa Pae field day: depart 08:00, survey, then at 15:00 the whole group takes the collecting and identification class while the helpers set the light trap, everyone together at the trap for dusk, back about 21:30. Nobody stays overnight at Pa Pae: the centre sleeps 20 and the group is 44. Day 3 is run by the pest control company — equipment, a short course on methods and precautions, outdoor injection and the underground reticulation pipes exposed — then certificates at CMU and the farewell party.
Participant model Drafted 40 participants: 25 students and 15 non-students — invited lecturers, pest management professionals, and visiting academics — plus 4 organising helpers, paid THB 600 per person per day, who are a cost rather than payers. Students’ rooms are booked for them as a block; non-students book their own or ask the organisers. Whether everyone pays the same fee is D1.
Participant handbook Drafted Programme, lecturers, field sites, kit list, safety rules, and fee information. Ready to publish once D1, D1b and D2 to D4 are settled; the fee field is currently blank. The handbook still has to be reissued for the Pa Pae day trip, the new session structure and the farewell party.
Field permissions and accommodation In progress The Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology, Chiang Mai University is proposed as co-organiser, subject to written confirmation of rooms and support. Mushroom Research Center access, sampling, specimen/data custody and publication terms also require a written agreement, and the THB 10,000 charge for laboratory use and evening hire of the grounds is the organisers’ working estimate, not yet a quotation from the centre. Which organisation runs Day 3 is not yet settled, and Mae Hia accommodation also remains to be confirmed.
Registration and payment webpage In progress Hor Kok Pheng has agreed to build and run a dedicated webpage that takes an applicant’s registration details and their payment — by credit card, Visa included — in one step. This replaces a bank-transfer process entirely: no wire charges, no proof-of-payment step, and confirmation is immediate. Not a board decision; reported here so the arrangement is on record.
Registration fee For board Blocked on D1 and D1b. Once the rate is set, Hor Kok Pheng’s page can go live and the announcement can go out.

05 — Why tonight matters

The critical path to 4 May 2027

Eight months remain. Roughly four of them are the recruitment window, and that window cannot open until the fee is set.

4 Sep 2026 — tonight Board approves the fee, the student grant, the registration timetable, CMU co-organiser credit, and the Mushroom Research Center agreement mandate.
Sep – Oct 2026 CMU account and receipting route arranged. Announcement and handbook published.
Oct 2026 Registration opens. Promotion through PRTRG members, member institutions, and regional entomological societies.
Nov 2026 – Feb 2027 Recruitment window, approximately four months, to fill 40 places — 25 students and 15 others. The 15 non-student places carry the margin.
28 Feb 2027 Registration closes. Deadline set by visa processing time, not by preference.
Mar 2027 Participants confirmed, visa invitation letters issued, field permissions finalised.
Apr 2027 Accommodation, transport, catering, and equipment committed. Refund window closes 31 March.
4–6 May 2027 1st PRTRG Termite Academy, Chiang Mai University.

Cost of deferral. If the fee decision moves to the next board meeting, the announcement slips with it and the recruitment window shrinks from roughly four months to less than three. For an international training activity where participants must arrange funding, leave, and visas, that is the difference between filling 40 places and filling only 31 — worth about THB 42,681 to the society.
