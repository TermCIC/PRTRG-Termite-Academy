Who does what

Pacific Rim Termite Research Group (PRTRG)

Organiser of the Academy

Provides
  • The programme and the lecturers
  • Registration, fees and the USD 100 student grant
  • Kit, T-shirt and travel insurance for every participant

Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology

Faculty of Agriculture, Chiang Mai University

Provides
  • Day 1 venue: lecture and laboratory rooms, audiovisual support, free of charge
  • Opening of the Academy by the Head of Department
  • Local academic coordination; Dr. Chun-I Chiu teaches termite taxonomy and the identification class

Mushroom Research Center

Pa Pae, Mae Taeng, Chiang Mai · mushroomresearchcentre.com

Provides
  • Day 2 field site, free of charge: soil digging, splitting deadwood and termite mounds, evening light trapping
  • Dr. Ishika Bera lectures on Day 1 on termite mushrooms in Thailand
Research outputs
New findings from the site are published with Kevin D. Hyde as co-author.
