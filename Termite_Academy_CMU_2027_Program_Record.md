## Program Purpose

The Termite Academy is proposed as a training workshop for students, young scholars, early-career termite researchers, and industry professionals in the Pacific Rim region.

The core purpose is to provide both academic and practical training in termite biology, termite survey methods, and termite management.

## Main Training Components

1. Indoor lectures covering basic termite biology
2. Field training on termite surveys
3. Practical training on termite management

## Termite Control Demonstration

The termite control / white ant control demonstration should be arranged with Suchart King Service.

This part should include a practical field site focused on house termite control. The demonstration should cover:

- house termite inspection
- termite control decision-making
- baiting system demonstration
- pesticide injection demonstration
- safety considerations for chemical use
- discussion of applied termite control practice in Thailand

This activity should be planned as an applied training component, not only a lecture. It will require a suitable house or building field site where termite activity or termite control points can be demonstrated safely.

## Participant Number Recommendation

Selected participant model: 25 trainees + 6 lecturers. Revised from 24 on 30 August 2026 when the accommodation decision was taken; Mae Hia has 34 beds, so 25 fits with margin.

This gives an expected core academic group of 30 people, excluding additional local organizing staff, drivers, assistants, or guests.

The most practical structure is:

- 24 trainees
- 6 lecturers
- 4 field groups
- 6 trainees per group
- at least 1 lecturer, mentor, or assistant connected to each group
- additional central staff for registration, transport, field safety, equipment, and overnight coordination

This size is suitable because the academy includes a full-day mountain trail survey at Sri Bua Ban campus, termite collection from mounds, soil, deadwood, and trees, overnight accommodation, light-trap setup, night observation, specimen handling, and safety supervision. Trail work in particular limits group size, because mentors must keep sight of their group along a route.

If the program accepts more than 24 trainees, the organizers should confirm:

- enough field mentors
- enough microscopes and lab space
- enough collection equipment
- sufficient transport capacity
- overnight accommodation capacity at or near Sri Bua Ban campus
- safety coverage for night work
- clear participant-to-mentor ratio

For the first academy, 24 trainees plus 6 lecturers is appropriate for training quality and field safety. A recommended public announcement wording would be: "Participation is limited to 24 trainees to ensure effective field training, hands-on supervision, and safety during overnight sampling."

## Date Clash: 4 May 2027 Is a Thai Public Holiday

### Finding

Researched 30 August 2026. **Tuesday 4 May 2027, the proposed Day 1 of the Academy, is Coronation Day (วันฉัตรมงคล), a Thai national public holiday.** It is also the final day of a four-day holiday weekend:

| Date | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday 1 May 2027 | National Labour Day |
| Sunday 2 May 2027 | Weekend |
| Monday 3 May 2027 | Substitute holiday for National Labour Day |
| Tuesday 4 May 2027 | Coronation Day - proposed Academy Day 1 |
| Wednesday 5 May 2027 | Normal working day |
| Thursday 6 May 2027 | Normal working day |

Coronation Day is fixed on 4 May every year and is not a lunar-calendar holiday, so this will not shift. The Bank of Thailand has listed 2027 as having eight long weekends, and this is one of them. The official Thai holiday calendar for 2027 is confirmed by cabinet in late 2026, but fixed-date holidays such as Coronation Day are certain.

### Why This Matters

- CMU administrative offices are closed. The Hariphunchai centre's own website states its office hours as Monday to Friday 08:30-16:30, closed weekends and public holidays.
- The opening ceremony on Day 1 would require CMU and PRTRG officials to attend on a national holiday.
- Catering, transport, AV, and technical staff may be unavailable or charge holiday rates.
- Registration, finance, and administrative support would not be available on Day 1.
- Chiang Mai is a major domestic destination. Over a four-day holiday weekend, flights and hotels into Chiang Mai are at peak demand and peak price, which works directly against keeping the Academy affordable for students.
- International participants arriving on 2 or 3 May would arrive into that peak.

### Options

| Option | Dates | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Shift by one day | Wednesday 5 - Friday 7 May 2027 | Smallest possible change. Clears the holiday entirely, keeps the same week and the same season. Ends Friday, so participants travel home over the weekend. Arrival on 4 May still lands in the holiday travel peak. |
| B. Shift by one week | Tuesday 11 - Thursday 13 May 2027 | A completely clean week with no holiday before, during, or after. Avoids the travel peak for arrivals as well. Still comfortably before Visakha Bucha on Thursday 20 May 2027. |
| C. Keep 4-6 May | Tuesday 4 - Thursday 6 May 2027 | Only viable if CMU confirms that offices, catering, transport, and the Hariphunchai centre will operate normally on the holiday, and if the opening ceremony can be staffed. Not recommended. |

Option B is the cleaner choice. It removes both the holiday clash and the peak-travel problem, and mid-May may be at least as good for dispersal flights as early May because flights are triggered by the first rains. The lecturers should be asked whether mid-May is equally suitable for the light-trap component before the change is made.

### Action

This must be raised at the PRTRG board meeting on 4 September 2026, because that meeting is expected to fix the dates. Recommending confirmation of 4-6 May without disclosing the holiday clash would commit the Academy to a date that is likely to cause operational problems and higher costs.

### Warning 1: Day 2 Runs Far Beyond a Standard Hire Day

The revised Day 2 begins with assembly at CMU at 06:45 and the night observation does not finish until about 21:30. That is roughly a **15-hour day against a 10-hour standard**, so about five hours of overtime per van, at THB 200 per hour each.

Whether this is charged depends on how the operator treats the middle of the day. If the vans are formally released after the 08:30 drop-off and re-engaged the next morning, overtime may not apply. If they are held on site all day and overnight, which is what the safety plan requires, it almost certainly will. **Get this written into the quotation rather than discovering it on the invoice.** At least one vehicle must remain on site overnight as the emergency vehicle, so at minimum one driver is on an extended day.

### Warning 2: Three Vans Is Probably Not Enough

A Toyota Commuter is commonly quoted as a 10-passenger vehicle. Three vans is therefore about 30 seats for a group of 30 people, with nothing left over for:

- 30 overnight bags
- light-trap equipment, sheets, poles, extension cables, and power supply
- collection kit, ethanol, vials, trays, first aid kits, and bulk drinking water
- local organising staff, field mentors, and the fieldwork coordinator, who are not part of the 30

The working headcount used for catering elsewhere in this record is approximately 35, not 30. Two options:

1. **Four vans** instead of three. Adds roughly THB 3,600-5,200 for two days plus overnight allowance and fuel.
2. **Three vans plus one pickup** for equipment and luggage. A pickup is considerably cheaper than a van, and it also solves the problem of carrying dirty field equipment and preserved specimens separately from passengers.

Option 2 is likely the better value and the better arrangement, because field equipment and ethanol should not travel in a passenger cabin.

### Why This Is a Better Field Site Scientifically

Pa Pae in Mae Taeng is forest and hill country rather than the farmland and scrub of the Mae Hia station. For a termite survey that is a straightforward improvement: more deadwood, more leaf litter, more intact habitat, and dispersal flights are more likely over forest than over cultivated ground.

A mushroom research centre is therefore an unusually apt place to teach termite biology, and if the centre works on Termitomyces at all, that is a lecture or a demonstration nobody else in the region could offer. Worth asking them directly.

## Field Sampling Location

All field sampling now takes place at Sri Bua Ban campus. The previously planned half-day survey on CMU main campus has been removed from the programme.

The Day 2 field programme has two parts, both at Sri Bua Ban:

1. A mountain trail survey through the morning and early afternoon
2. Light-trap setup, night observation of dispersal flights, and an overnight stay

The mountain trail survey will focus on sampling termites from mounds, soil, deadwood, leaf litter, and trees along the trail. Working a trail through changing elevation and habitat gives trainees a wider range of termite microhabitats in one transect than a campus survey would, but it also removes the easy, close-to-facilities warm-up that the CMU main campus survey previously provided.

This location supports:

- termite sampling across a trail transect
- field survey practice in forest and hill habitat
- light-trap setup and monitoring
- observation of termite dispersal flights, depending on weather and seasonal conditions
- overnight field training experience

The overnight activity requires planning for accommodation, transport, safety, field permissions, lighting/electricity or battery power, equipment security, emergency contacts, and participant supervision.

### Consequences of Removing the CMU Main Campus Survey

- Trainees no longer have a low-difficulty practice survey before the harder trail work. The first fieldwork of the Academy is now a half-day on a mountain trail. Day 1 Lecture 4 on survey methods and the Day 1 field briefing therefore carry more weight, because they are the only preparation trainees receive.
- The specimen labelling scheme simplifies: there is no longer a need to distinguish CMU main campus material from Sri Bua Ban material. Labels can instead distinguish trail collection points along the transect.
- Permission to sample on CMU main campus is no longer required, which removes one approval from the critical path.
- Day 2 now starts before 07:00 and ends after 21:00. This is a long day and the schedule has no slack; a late departure or a slow trail directly threatens the 15:00 light-trap setup.
